    The five bands

    Our name is a colour gamut. So is the diagnostic.

    Every institution has range. The question is which part of it has gone quiet — and whether anyone in the building has said so out loud.

    Purpose

    Can everyone say why you exist, the same way?

    Alignment

    Does your board agree on what to stop doing?

    Momentum

    Can a decision travel from the room to the work?

    Relevance

    Do the people you need next know you exist?

    Capacity

    Can you sustain the version of you that’s coming?
